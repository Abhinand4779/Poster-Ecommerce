Shipping Policy

At Klaiz Designs, we understand that the anticipation of receiving your order is part of the excitement. We prioritize swift and reliable shipping for all our online poster purchases.

1. Order Processing Time

  • Orders are processed within 2 business days.
  • For custom posters, processing time may vary based on design complexity and current order volume.

2. Shipping Time

DestinationDelivery Time
🇮🇳 Domestic (India)5–10 Business Days
🌍 International15–20 Business Days (varies by location)

3. Delivery

  • We ensure doorstep delivery for all orders.
  • If courier services are unavailable in your location, you may need to collect the package from the nearest courier office.
  • A tracking ID will be shared via WhatsApp and email once the order is shipped.

4. Shipping Delays

While we strive for timely delivery, shipping delays caused by courier services are beyond our control. In such cases, we appreciate your patience and understanding.

5. International Shipping

Shipping rates, delivery times, and applicable taxes or duties for international shipments are the buyer's responsibility.

6. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If a shipment is damaged or lost in transit, contact us immediately with photos or videos as proof. We will resolve the issue as quickly as possible.
