Help Center

How long does delivery take?

We typically dispatch orders within 2 business days. Delivery takes 3–5 business days depending on your location. Metro cities usually receive orders faster.

Can I track my order?

Yes, once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via email and WhatsApp. You can also track it from your Account Dashboard.

What paper quality do you use?

We use premium 300 GSM gallery-grade paper. You can choose between a Matte finish for a classic look or a Glossy finish for high-contrast, vibrant colors.

Can I return a poster?

We accept returns for damaged or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery. Since custom posters are made to order, they are non-returnable unless defective.

How do I upload a custom photo?

Go to our Custom Builder page, upload your high-resolution image (JPG/PNG), select your size, and preview it on a virtual wall before ordering.

How do I place a bulk order?

For orders of 20+ posters, please contact our business team at bulk@klaizdesigns.com for special pricing and expedited shipping options.

Still need help?

Our team is available Mon–Sat, 10 AM to 7 PM.
